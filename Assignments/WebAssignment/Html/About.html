Rashmika Mandanna

Name: Rashmika Mandanna
Born: 5 April 1996, Virajpet, Karnataka, India
Profession: Actress, Model
Industries: Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, Hindi Cinema
Nickname: National Crush of India


Education

Rashmika Mandanna completed her schooling at Coorg Public School.
She pursued a degree in Psychology, Journalism and English Literature.
Her academic background helped shape her confident personality.


Career Highlights

Rashmika gained popularity with movies like Kirik Party and Geetha Govindam.
She is known for her expressive acting and charming screen presence.
She has worked across multiple film industries in India.
